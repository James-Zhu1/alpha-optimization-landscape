document asks one narrower question: how has the expression that study selected
behaved on data that did not exist when it was chosen?

The study's holdout ended at the configured `end_date`. The rows below score the
already-selected expression on observations after that boundary, so no part of this
window informed the search, the selection, or the audit. Nothing here selects,
tunes, or reranks anything — that is what keeps it an audit rather than a second
experiment.

This window grows every time the harness is rerun. Until the interval narrows enough

which is a statement about sample size, not a verdict on the signal.
